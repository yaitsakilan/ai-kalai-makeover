1. Executive Brief & Redesign Mission

Overview

Kalai Makeover is a premier luxury bridal makeover studio, beauty parlour, and professional training academy located in Tamil Nadu, India. The application is an all-in-one management ERP powered by AI that runs the complete day-to-day operations of the enterprise.

🎯 The Core Mission for the UI/UX Redesigner
Transform the current functional MVP prototype into a high-end luxury, mobile-first SaaS experience that feels as elegant and glamorous as a high-fashion beauty atelier, while remaining blazing fast and effortless for staff during busy salon rush hours.

Key Objectives of the Redesign

Design Pillar Current Pain Point Redesign Target Experience
1. Luxury Aesthetics Dark mode looks functional but generic; typography and spacing feel technical. High-fashion bridal atelier vibe: warm noir background, champagne gold highlights, Playfair/Cormorant serif headlines, soft glassmorphism.
2. Mobile-First Ergonomics Dense data tables overflow on small mobile screens; requires horizontal scrolling. Adaptive card stacks, bottom action sheet drawers, floating action buttons (FAB), and thumb-friendly checkout flows.
3. Sub-15s Quick Action Filling salon walk-in details takes multiple form fields and keystrokes. 1-tap service chips, auto-calculated service tallies, smart phone formatting, and bilingual Tanglish AI voice wizard.
4. Role-Based Clarity Staff and Owner share navigation menus, requiring permission warnings. Completely segregated views: Owner Command Center vs Simplified Staff Self-Service (punch-in & booking only).

3. Roles & Information Architecture

Security & IA

The platform operates under strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). The UI/UX designer must design two distinct experience pathways:

👑 1. Studio Owner Experience (Kalai)
Full Control
Accessible via Secure PIN (kalai1610). Unrestricted access to all business secrets, financials, and staff controls.
  • Executive Dashboard & 7-Day Bridal countdown
  • Full Customer CRM & repeat visit history
  • Bridal Events pipeline & Subha Muhurtham calendar
  • Shop expenses, product stock & bill scanner (OCR)
  • Academy student tuition & installment ledger
  • Jewellery rental catalog & asset ROI tracking
  • HR management: Staff WiFi attendance & payroll slips
  • P&L Statements, Cash vs GPay balance & Net Profit
👤 2. Employee Self-Service Experience
Restricted
Accessible via Staff PIN (emp123) or personal password. Distraction-free, zero financial visibility.
  • 1-Tap WiFi Attendance Clock-In & Clock-Out
  • Quick Shop Customer Check-In (New walk-ins)
  • Quick Student Class Enrollment
  • Assigned Bridal Events schedule (call times & venues)
  • Personal monthly attendance sheet & leave tracker
  • Personal monthly payslip view & download
  • Strictly Blocked: Financial totals, P&L, Jewel purchase costs, and Owner analytics

4. Exhaustive Feature Specifications (11 Modules)

Core Modules
📊 Module 1: Unified Executive Dashboard & Real-Time Pulse
Owner Only
The high-impact command center summarizing business health in under 5 seconds.
  • Financial Metric Cards: Gross Revenue, Total Outflows, Net Operating Profit, and Cash Drawer vs GPay/Bank Account balance breakdown.
  • Upcoming Events & Bridal Countdown (7 Days): Dynamic cards displaying scheduled brides with priority countdown tags (TODAY! 👑, Tomorrow 🔥, In 3 Days), service package, venue, and balance due.
  • Pending Payment Radar: Immediate alert queue of customers with unpaid balances across salon visits and bridal bookings, with 1-click WhatsApp reminder triggers.
  • Daily Business Logging Streak Widget (🔥): Visual streak tracker showing consecutive days of shop activity logging to ensure consistent record-keeping.
  • Quick Action Command Bar: Instant modal triggers for + Add Shop Customer, + Book Event, + Add Expense, + Enroll Student, and + Jewel Rental.
  • Monthly Trajectory Visualizer: Chart.js revenue vs expense comparative graph.
💇‍♀️ Module 2: Shop Customer Management & Service POS
High Frequency
Daily parlour walk-in management, customer profiling, and repeat customer retention.
  • Customer Profile & Ordinal Visit Counter: Tracks lifetime visit history with ordinal loyalty tags (1st Visit, 2nd Visit, 5th Visit VIP).
  • Service POS Multi-Selector: Chip-based service selection (Facial, Haircut, Hair Spa, Bleach, Waxing, Threading, Pedicure, Saree Draping) with auto-sum total calculation.
  • Staff Attendant Tagging: Assigns which beautician serviced the client for individual performance metrics and wage commission.
  • Payment Split Engine: Handles Cash, GPay/UPI, Card, or Multi-split payments with paid/pending status flags.
  • Segmentation & Retention Tabs: Segment clients by All History, Top Paying (High LTV), Repeat Clients, Lapsed Clients (30+ days inactive), and New Walk-ins.
  • Automated WhatsApp Touchpoints: 1-click WhatsApp triggers for: Service Invoice / Thank You, Review Request, and "We Miss You" reactivation discounts.
  • Cross-Module Quick Convert: Turn any customer into an Event Booking or Academy Student with 1 click.
👰 Module 3: Bridal & Event Booking + Subha Muhurtham Calendar
High Ticket
High-value wedding booking management integrated with the Hindu/Tamil astrological calendar.
  • Subha Muhurtham Engine: Curated 2025–2027 Tamil Nadu Muhurtham database + live Nitya Panchangam API (Tithi, Nakshatram, Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam) highlighting peak bridal dates.
  • Month Booking Calendar Overview: Visual monthly calendar grid showing booked event density per day to prevent overbooking.
  • Event Financial Calculator: Agreed package price, advance paid, balance due, travel allowance (TA), assistant artist staff wages, and net event profit.
  • Logistics & Timing Tracker: Call-sheet / makeup start time, ceremony muhurtham time, Kalyana Mandapam venue map link, assigned makeup artist, and bridal saree draping notes.
  • Status Pipeline: Confirmed, Pending Advance, In Progress, Completed, Overdue Payment, and Cancelled.
  • Overdue Status & Quick Finish: 1-click action to mark past events as completed and trigger final settlement.
  • WhatsApp Bridal Kit: Instant booking confirmation letters, advance receipts, and venue arrival reminders.
🧾 Module 4: Expenses, Inventory & Supply Chain
Financial
Categorized shop expenditure tracking and monthly cash/bank balance reconciliation.
  • 3-Way Categorized Expense Forms:
    1. Salon Products: Hair colors, developer, facial kits, bleach, shampoos, and salon inventory.
    2. Makeup & Accessories: False lashes, hair extensions, setting sprays, pins, vanity accessories.
    3. General Overhead: Salon rent, TNEB electricity, water bills, cleaning, travel, and miscellaneous.
  • Monthly Balance Reconciliation: Opening balance, physical Cash at salon drawer, GPay / Bank account balance, and closing balance.
  • Vendor Expense Analytics: Category-wise pie charts and vendor-specific spending trends over time.
📸 Module 5: OCR Smart Bill & Invoice Scanner
AI Powered
Camera/gallery receipt scanner that extracts line items and automatically enters shop expenses.
  • Camera & Photo Upload: Direct camera capture from mobile phone or gallery image upload.
  • AI Vision / Whisper Extraction: Automatically detects Store Name, Bill Date, Individual Items, Subtotals, and Grand Total.
  • 1-Click Expense Conversion: Saves scanned bills directly into the product expenses ledger without manual typing.
  • Digital Receipt Vault: Stores digital receipts and audit history for tax and accounting reconciliation.
🎓 Module 6: Makeup Academy & Student Tuition Ledger
Academy
Student admissions, batch scheduling, and installment payment tracking.
  • Course Catalog: Professional Bridal Masterclass, Self-Grooming, Hair Styling & Draping, Nail Art, and Certification courses.
  • Student Profiles: Full name, contact number, batch timing, enrollment date, and course selected.
  • Milestone Installment Tracker: Total agreed fee, installment payments logged (Cash/GPay), and pending balance.
  • Fee Reminder System: 1-click WhatsApp payment reminders with remaining tuition balance details.
  • Status Pipeline: Active, Course Completed, Certified, or On-Hold.
💎 Module 7: Bridal Jewellery Rental & Asset ROI Tracker
Asset Management
Bridal ornament rental inventory, rental ledgers, and return deposit management.
  • Jewel Catalog & Photo Vault: Chokers, Harams, Oddiyanams (hip belts), Jhumkas, Maang Tikkas, and Hair sets with purchase price and photos.
  • Rental Ledger: Customer name, rental issue date, expected return date, rental fee charged, and refundable security deposit.
  • Return Inspection Workflow: 1-click return settlement, deposit refund, or damage fee deduction.
  • Cumulative Asset ROI Calculator: Tracks total rental income generated vs initial acquisition cost (shows when an ornament has broken even and is generating pure profit).
👥 Module 8: HR Staff Management & Geofenced WiFi Attendance
HR & Payroll
Staff database, biometric-grade WiFi attendance tracking, and monthly payslip generation.
  • Complete Staff KYC: Designation (Senior Artist, Beautician, Hair Stylist, Trainee), emergency phone, Aadhaar card number, photo, bank account number & IFSC.
  • Salon WiFi Router Binding: Captures the salon's physical WiFi router network IP to enforce onsite presence.
  • Geofenced Punch In / Punch Out: Staff can ONLY clock in when connected to the shop WiFi network (prevents proxy or remote check-ins).
  • Automated Attendance Timesheet: Present, Late, Half-Day, Absent, On-Leave status with automated work-hour tally.
  • Automated Payroll Engine: Base monthly salary, attendance loss-of-pay deductions, event incentives, salary advance deductions, and Net Salary Payable.
  • PDF Payslip Generator: 1-click downloadable, formal salary slip with salon branding.
📱 Module 9: Employee Mobile Self-Service Portal
Mobile Portal
Simplified, distraction-free mobile screen for salon staff on the floor.
  • 1-Tap WiFi Punch Button: Big, clear clock-in/out button with immediate network validation feedback.
  • Quick Customer Entry: Simplified modal for staff to log parlour clients without accessing owner screens.
  • Quick Class Enrollment: Enroll academy walk-ins quickly.
  • Personal Timesheet & Leave Balance: View personal monthly attendance calendar and remaining leave days.
  • Personal Payslip Download: Access individual monthly salary slips securely.
  • Strict Data Isolation: Shop revenue, net profit, jewel costs, and owner analytics remain completely invisible.
🎙️ Module 10: Multilingual AI Voice Assistant (Tamil / Tanglish)
AI Voice
Whisper v3 + Groq AI voice wizard for hands-free, zero-typing entry in Tamil and Tanglish.
  • Bilingual Voice Recognition: Accurately understands spoken English, pure Tamil, and colloquial Tanglish (mixed Tamil-English).
  • Automated Variable Extraction: Spoken commands like "Priya 9876543210 Chennai facial 1500 rating 5" are automatically parsed into Name, Phone, Location, Services, Amount, and Rating.
  • Zero-Typing Database Insert: Submits customer visits, event bookings, and expenses directly into the database.
  • Voice Overlay & Visualizer: Floating microphone button with pulsating audio waves and real-time transcription display.
  • Conversational Business Intelligence: Ask spoken queries (e.g. "How many bridal bookings do we have next month?") and receive instant answers.
💰 Module 11: Financial Statements, P&L & Business Analytics
Accounting
Formal Profit & Loss accounting, cash flow balancing, and visual sales intelligence.
  • Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement: Aggregated Gross Inflow across all 4 revenue streams (Shop, Events, Academy, Jewels) minus Total Operating Expenses.
  • Liquidity Balancing: Exact physical Cash Drawer count vs Bank Account / GPay balance.
  • Monthly & All-Time Historical Filter: Jump between individual months or view lifetime business metrics.
  • Export to PDF: 1-click printable financial balance sheet for tax filing and audits.
  • Service & Revenue Trend Charts: Visual rankings of high-margin services, popular salon treatments, and monthly sales trajectories.

5. Critical User Interaction Flows

UX Flows

The UI/UX designer must map out and optimize these 5 high-frequency interaction workflows:

Flow A: 15-Second Salon Customer Entry (The Rush-Hour Flow)
1
Tap "+ Add" Customer: Bottom nav FAB or top navbar button opens a lightweight bottom drawer sheet.
2
Input Name & Phone: Auto-formats 10-digit mobile number with phone validation; checks for previous visits.
3
Tap Service Chips: Tapping Facial + Threading auto-adds prices (₹1500 + ₹100 = ₹1600).
4
Select Staff & Payment: Tap beautician pill and payment mode (Cash / GPay). Tap Save & Send WhatsApp.
Flow B: Bridal Booking with Subha Muhurtham Date Selection
1
Open Event Booking: Tap "Book Event" from Events module or Muhurtham calendar widget.
2
Date Picker with Muhurtham Badges: Calendar displays gold dots on auspicious Muhurtham dates with live Panchangam details.
3
Financial Split: Enter agreed package (e.g. ₹35,000), advance collected (₹10,000) -> Auto-computes Balance (₹25,000).
4
Logistics & Staff: Enter ceremony & call-sheet timings, Kalyana Mandapam address, and assign assistant artist.
Flow C: Geofenced WiFi Staff Clock-In / Clock-Out
1
Staff Arrives & Connects to Salon WiFi: Opens mobile portal; app automatically checks connected network IP.
2
Network Match Check: Green badge "Connected to Salon WiFi Router" activates the Clock-In button.
3
1-Tap Punch In: Logs exact timestamp, marked as Present or Late (if after 9:30 AM).
